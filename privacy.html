Privacy Policy

Lingua Journey · Last updated 19 August 2026

Lingua Journey lets you hold a spoken conversation with an AI practice partner and then shows you what you learned. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone when you do that, and what does not.

The short version. There is no account and no sign-up. Your practice history stays on your device. Your voice is sent to an AI model while a mission is running, because that is the only way it can answer you — and it is not stored on our server afterwards.

What we collect while you practise

  • Your microphone audio, only while a mission is running. The app does not listen at any other time.
  • The text of what you said, transcribed from that audio so your partner can reply and so we can prepare your review afterwards.
  • Which mission you chose, and how long the session lasted.

Where it goes

Your audio travels to our relay server, and from there to Alibaba Cloud's Qwen model service, which generates your partner's speech and your session review. The relay is hosted in mainland China. Alibaba Cloud processes the audio and text under its own terms; we do not control how long it holds them.

Nothing else receives your data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What our server keeps

No audio and no transcript is ever written to disk on our server. It passes through memory and is gone when the session ends.

Our server logs record only what is needed to keep the service running and to stop abuse: the type of event, a request identifier, the mission identifier, a status code, how many milliseconds it took, and the first eight characters of the anonymous identifier described below. No part of your conversation appears in them.

The identifier

To apply a fair usage limit per person, the app generates a random value the first time you launch it and sends it with each request. It is a random number, hashed — not your device identifier, advertising identifier, phone number, or anything derived from your hardware. Nothing about you or your phone can be recovered from it. Deleting and reinstalling the app creates a completely new one, which we have no way to connect to the old one.

What stays on your phone

Your practice records, the transcripts of your sessions, and the learning cards generated from them are stored on your device and are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We cannot recover it for you, and we cannot read it.

Children

Lingua Journey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings. Live conversation is the app's core function, so it will not work without it.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored.
  • Write to us at the address below with any question about your data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Where a change materially affects what leaves your phone, we will say so in the app.

隐私政策

Lingua Journey · 最后更新 2026 年 8 月 19 日

Lingua Journey 让你和 AI 伙伴进行真实的语音对话，结束后告诉你学到了什么。这一页说明在这个过程中，究竟有哪些数据离开你的手机，以及哪些没有。

一句话版本。不需要注册，没有账号。你的练习记录只留在你自己的手机上。对话进行时你的声音会被发送给 AI 模型——否则它无法回应你——但结束后不会保存在我们的服务器上。

练习时我们收集什么

  • 麦克风音频，仅在任务进行中。其他任何时候 app 都不会收音。
  • 你所说内容的文字，由音频转写而来，用于让伙伴回应你，以及生成你的复盘。
  • 你选择了哪个任务，以及本次时长。

数据流向哪里

你的音频先到我们的中转服务器，再转发给阿里云通义千问（Qwen）模型服务，由它生成伙伴的语音回应和你的学习复盘。中转服务器位于中国大陆。阿里云依其自身条款处理这些音频与文本，其保留时长不在我们控制之内。

除此之外没有任何第三方接收你的数据。app 内没有广告、没有统计分析、也没有任何第三方 SDK。

我们的服务器保留什么

音频和文字记录从不会写入我们服务器的磁盘。它们只经过内存，会话结束即消失。

服务器日志只记录维持服务与防止滥用所必需的内容：事件类型、请求编号、任务编号、状态码、耗时毫秒数，以及下文所述匿名标识的前八位。对话内容不会出现在日志中的任何位置。

关于那个标识

为了按人施加公平的用量上限，app 在首次启动时生成一个随机值并随请求发送。它是一个随机数的哈希——不是设备标识、广告标识、手机号，也不是从硬件推导出来的任何东西。无法从中还原出关于你或你手机的任何信息。删除后重装会生成一个全新的值，我们没有任何办法把它和旧的关联起来。

留在手机上的部分

你的练习记录、对话文字记录、以及由此生成的学习卡，都存储在你的设备上，不会上传到任何地方。删除 app 即全部清除。我们无法为你恢复，也无法读取。

儿童

Lingua Journey 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在知情的情况下收集他们的信息。

你的选择

  • 随时在 iOS「设置」中撤销麦克风权限。实时对话是本 app 的核心功能，撤销后将无法使用。
  • 删除 app 即可清除它保存的一切。

变更
